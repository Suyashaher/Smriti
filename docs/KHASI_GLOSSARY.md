## 2. Linguistic and Orthographic Conventions

1. **Orthography and Diacritics**:
   - The Khasi alphabet utilizes the Latin script with specific accented characters, most notably **`ï`** (distinct from *i*) and **`ñ`** (palatal nasal).
   - In digital localization catalogs, UTF-8 character encoding must be strictly preserved to prevent mojibake corruption of characters like `ï` and `ñ`.

2. **Tone & Register for Elderly Users**:
   - Khasi vocabulary selected for elderly patients with cognitive impairment must prioritize **colloquial familiarity and warmth** over high formal literature registers.
   - Greetings such as *"Phi long kumno"* ("How are you?") provide an empathetic, universally understood greeting across Khasi sub-dialects (Sohra, Shillong, Bhoi, War).

3. **Validation Guidelines**:
   - A qualified native speaker from Meghalaya should evaluate each term for cultural resonance, age appropriateness, and dialectal neutrality.
   - Any modifications suggested during field validation must be reflected in both `KHASI_GLOSSARY.md` and `frontend/src/i18n/kh.json`.
